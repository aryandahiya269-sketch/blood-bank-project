Who can donate?

  • Age 18 to 65 years
  • Weight above 50 kg
  • Haemoglobin 12.5 g/dL+
  • No fever or infection
  • 3 months since last donation
  • No recent tattoo or surgery

A short health check is done at the centre before every donation.

Common questions

Is donating blood safe?

Yes. A new sterile kit is used for every donor. Your body replaces the donated blood within a few weeks.

How often can I donate?

Men can donate every 3 months and women every 4 months, provided they pass the health check.

How long does it take?

The donation takes about 10 minutes. Registration and screening make the whole visit around 45 minutes.

How do I get blood for a patient?

Submit the request form above with the patient and hospital details. If stock is available, units are reserved immediately.
